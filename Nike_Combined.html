Stage 02 of 05
Validation & Matching

"The invoices that make it through capture still face a gauntlet. Your team described a process where tolerance mismatches stop everything, buyers are pulled into GL coding decisions, and reclassification happens after the fact — routinely."

— From the room · April 7, 2026
💡
The hidden cost you haven't fully measured yet
Your buyers are spending real time manually coding GL accounts for non-PO invoices. That work has nothing to do with their jobs — and reclassification after the fact doubles the cost of every one of those decisions. This is entirely eliminable.
What you told us
Your validation reality
2-decimal rounding stops — anything off by cents halts your flow
Low first-pass yield as a result
Manual exception handling via email chains — no structured workflow
Your buyers own non-PO GL coding — it's creating confusion and inconsistency
Reclassification after the fact — every recode is double the work
Segmented compliance rules across your regions
One-source tax direct, manual tax allocation indirect — regional complexity
What changes with Basware
AP Automation — Matching Engine
2-way, 3-way, n-way PO matching with configurable tolerances — your 2-decimal stops become a tuning parameter, not a blocker.
Smart Coding
AI auto-suggests GL codes for every non-PO invoice. Your buyers are freed from coding decisions. Reclassification cycles stop.
AP Assurance
Automated compliance — tax rules, country mandates, vendor master integrity — enforced before invoices advance. Your segmented compliance problem becomes a configuration.
⚖ Compliance
Regional e-invoicing regulations enforced during validation. Indirect tax allocation handled by rules, not by people.

Stage 03 of 05
Approval Workflow

"Two days. That's your average approval cycle. Not because your approvers are slow — but because routing is inconsistent across regions, tracking lives in a separate system, and nobody has a single view of where anything is."

— From the room · April 7, 2026
⏱
Your 2-day problem is a routing problem, not a people problem
You use a separate system for tracking approvals — but uptake varies by region, workflow maturity varies by team, and no one can baseline the cycle time with confidence. Standardising routing alone could cut this dramatically — without asking anyone to work faster.
What you told us
Your approval reality
Average 2-day approval cycle — with no consistent baseline to improve against
Your approval tracking system (APP) is used inconsistently across regions
Varying workflow maturity by country — no single governed model
Multiple touches required before matching can proceed
Country-specific routing rules add layers of complexity
Capability approval flows being flagged as legal issues
What changes with Basware
AP Automation — Routing Engine
Configurable rules-based approval chains — delegation, escalation, mobile approval. One governed model that replaces your regional patchwork.
Smart Workflow
Advanced conditional logic, SLA enforcement, and multi-org rule sets — all the nuance your regions need, built into one system.
AI Business Agent
Chases approvals autonomously. Escalates stalled invoices before they blow out cycle time. Surfaces bottleneck insights so your team can act, not just react.
